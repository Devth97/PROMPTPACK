A whole-batch example in INR

Why does a small quantity change add another batch?

The batch count rounds up: ceil(order quantity ÷ recipe yield). Fourteen cupcakes from a recipe yielding twelve require two whole batches in this model. If your actual production process combines orders or can safely make a partial recipe, review a separate allocation instead of assuming spare units will sell.

Can I use this as a cookie pricing calculator?

Yes, if the yield and order use the same unit and your batch costs describe that cookie recipe. For a whole cake, make one unit mean the whole cake in both fields. This exercise does not convert grams, cups, servings or recipe yields.

How do I cost each ingredient?

For compatible units, use purchase-pack cost ÷ pack quantity × recipe usage. A 1,000 g pack costing ₹60 contributes ₹15 when the recipe uses 250 g. Sum the recipe's ingredient lines to get the batch figure entered here.
