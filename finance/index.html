04FAQ

Questions.

What do I receive?

A 29-page PDF: Gmail setup for Codex and Claude Code, connection tests, a master Gmail audit prompt, evidence ledger, subscription review, overlap, annual-cost, renewal, fee and cancellation checks, competitor comparison and negotiation prompts, a 10-minute quick run, a re-audit prompt and security guardrails.

What do I need to use it?

An AI agent that supports connectors — Codex or Claude Code — a Gmail account that holds your billing emails, and a Gmail connector (MCP) you trust that supports read-only Google sign-in. The pack walks you through setup.

Does PromptPack access my Gmail or bank?

No. You connect Gmail to your own AI tool through a connector you choose. PromptPack never receives your emails and never connects to your bank or accounts.

Is it safe to connect Gmail?

The pack recommends read-only, least-privilege access and tells the AI to treat every email as untrusted data. Connectors are made by third parties, so review one before you grant access. You can revoke access any time in your Google account. No setup is risk-free.

Does it cancel or send anything automatically?

No. It drafts messages and cancellation steps for you to review. Nothing is sent, cancelled, paid or changed without your explicit approval.

Does it guarantee savings?

No. Results depend on your situation and what your email contains — Gmail may not show every transaction. The pack helps you look more closely; it promises no amount saved.

Can I use ChatGPT or Gemini instead?

The pack is built around Codex and Claude Code because they support Gmail connectors. Other tools with similar connector support may work, but they are not covered in the guide.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a set of prompts for organisation, research and drafting — not financial, legal or tax advice. AI output can be wrong, so check anything important yourself.

How is it delivered?

Instantly after payment: you land on a page with a secure download link, and we may also email you a copy. Nothing is shipped.
