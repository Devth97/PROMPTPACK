AI Agent Deleted Your Database? What to Do First

Stop anything that is still writing to the database, then look for your host’s automatic backups or point-in-time restore. Restore into a new database, check it, and only then switch your app over. Next time, give AI agents a copy of the data, never your production password.

By PromptPack · Reviewed 28 October 2026

What should you do, step by step?

  1. Stop the writes. Pause the agent, the app or any job that still connects to the database. New writes can overwrite what a restore would recover.
  2. Find your recovery options. In your database host’s dashboard, look for backups, snapshots or point-in-time restore, and note the most recent time before the deletion. Options and retention differ by host and plan; check its documentation.
  3. Restore beside, not over. Where your host allows it, restore into a new database first. Check that the tables and recent records you need are there.
  4. Switch and record. Point the app at the restored database, then write down what happened and what was lost.
  5. Prevent the repeat. Give AI agents a development copy and credentials that cannot delete production data. Require your approval for destructive commands, and test a restore before you need one.

A general first response, not advice for a specific host. If there is no backup and no point-in-time restore, the data may not be recoverable; contact your host’s support quickly.
