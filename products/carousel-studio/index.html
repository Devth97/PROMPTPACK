Six practical workshop steps

  1. Choose one reader and one job
  2. Study references as a designer
  3. Lock a system, not six layouts
  4. Make the cover earn the swipe
  5. Write a complete image prompt
  6. Review the output, not the prompt

Your first session

  1. Read GUIDE.pdf, then fill MASTER-BRIEF.md and collect verified source notes.
  2. Study references with REFERENCE-WORKSHEET.csv, then plan every slide in WORKSHEET.csv.
  3. Run the staged prompts in PROMPTS.md: choose an angle, lock the visual system and storyboard six slides.
  4. Generate one cover in your image tool, check it at phone size, then generate the interiors and review them with QUALITY-CHECKLIST.md.
