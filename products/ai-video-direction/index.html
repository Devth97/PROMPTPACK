Six practical workshop steps

  1. Choose an observable visual promise
  2. Make a reference and continuity contract
  3. Give camera and subject separate jobs
  4. Adapt the prompt to the production route
  5. Choose takes by continuity before beauty
  6. Deliver a cut-ready direction package

Your first session

  1. Choose a simple subject and read the six-shot amber-room treatment; fill your own master brief.
  2. Approve one subject reference and lock geometry, material, light and camera axis.
  3. Use the staged prompts to prototype one camera move, record actual model settings and inspect the complete clip.
  4. Select takes through the continuity gate and give an editor matching shot IDs, usable ranges, rights notes and the edit map.
