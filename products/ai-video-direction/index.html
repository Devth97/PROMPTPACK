Try an editable prompt

Replace the bracketed fields with your own project details. This sample is part of the included prompt library.

Turn this brief into a shot specification: [BRIEF]. Use only these product facts: [FACTS]. Describe subject identity, action, camera position, lens feel, lighting, background and duration in separate fields. Make the action physically plausible. Define start and end frames and list details that must stay constant across takes. Provide a shorter fallback prompt if the generator ignores the main action. Finish with a review checklist for geometry, text, hands, reflections and continuity. Do not claim that any take has been generated or tested.
