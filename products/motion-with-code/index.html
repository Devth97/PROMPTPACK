Questions

Is this an AI video generator?

No. Every frame is drawn by code (three.js in a browser) and rendered on your own computer. The only AI involved is the voices, and the free ones run locally. That is why a reel looks the same every time and one changed line re-renders cleanly.

Do I need to know how to code?

No, but you need to be comfortable installing a few free tools (Node.js, Python, ffmpeg) and pasting commands. A coding assistant like Claude Code, Codex or Cursor makes it easier: the guide gives you the exact prompts.

Is it really unlimited?

Yes. The engine is yours, so there are no credits, no per-video fee and no watermark. With the free voices, each reel costs nothing. Premium ElevenLabs voices are optional and use your own ElevenLabs plan.

How long does a reel take?

Writing is up to you. Rendering a 35-second reel took about 4 to 5 minutes on our laptop; yours depends on your computer. You can queue a whole week of episodes with one command.

Can I use my own characters?

Yes. The pack includes a character design sheet, a voice casting sheet and a brand reskin guide, so you can make your own mascot and colours.
