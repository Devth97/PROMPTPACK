Your first session

Start with one product.

Open the separate demo to learn the layout, then use the blank workbook with your own figures. Start small before logging the rest of your business.

  1. Gather your costs. Use a recent selling price, landed product cost, packaging and actual shipping or gateway charges.
  2. Enter one SKU. Review its contribution before ads, then compare it with the acquisition cost from your records.
  3. Test one assumption. Change a shipping or COD/RTO assumption and compare the result. A scenario is an estimate, not a forecast.
  4. Build your record. Add orders and campaign figures, then review the monthly summary alongside your actual settlements.

Frequently asked questions

Does ProfitPulse automatically connect to Meta Ads or Shopify?

No. It is a manual Excel toolkit. You enter actual order, cost and campaign figures yourself.

Does it include GST accounting?

It includes configurable basic tax assumptions, but it is not a GST returns, invoicing or statutory accounting solution. Verify tax treatment with a qualified professional.

Is it guaranteed to improve my ROAS or profit?

No. It helps you analyze assumptions and track outcomes; results depend on your data and business decisions.

Can I use Google Sheets?

Excel is recommended. If importing into Sheets, verify formulas and formatting before relying on the results.
