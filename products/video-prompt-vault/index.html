Six practical workshop steps

  1. Select by editorial job
  2. Translate the reference into a system
  3. Write one movement hypothesis
  4. Adapt a library brief to the tool
  5. Review the clip through time
  6. Make a reusable selected-shot menu

Your first session

  1. Read the thirty retained shot briefs and choose one editorial gap in your project.
  2. Shortlist three candidates, approve the subject reference and translate the visual system.
  3. Adapt one brief to your chosen provider, generate a modest motion test and log actual settings.
  4. Inspect the entire clip, choose a fallback if needed and hand off the selected shot with usable ranges and rights notes.
