About PromptPack

PromptPack sells ready-to-use AI prompt packs — digital products for the AI tools you already use. The AI Money Audit Prompt Pack is a 29-page guide and prompt system: you connect Gmail (read-only) to Codex or Claude Code, and the AI agent reviews your billing emails to surface recurring expenses, subscriptions, alternatives and negotiation opportunities.

It is a one-time digital purchase. There is no subscription and no account to create. PromptPack never receives your emails, does not connect to your bank, and does not move money, cancel services or make purchases for you.
