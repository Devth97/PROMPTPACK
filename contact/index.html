About PromptPack

PromptPack sells ready-to-use AI prompt packs — digital products you paste into the AI tool you already use. The AI Money Audit Prompt Pack is a reusable prompt system for reviewing recurring expenses, subscriptions, bills, alternatives and negotiation opportunities.

It is a one-time digital purchase. There is no subscription and no account to create. PromptPack does not connect to your bank, move money, cancel services or make purchases for you.
