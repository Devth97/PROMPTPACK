## Intent

The flagship demo reel for PromptPack's "Motion Briefs for Code" pack, which is being upgraded into a code-animated character reel system (price ₹1,499 / US$29). Two original low-poly characters on a laptop keyboard explain, as a short comedic dialogue, how reels like this one are made with code instead of a camera or a video model: script, voices, shot plan, render, mix. It must feel like a real, scroll-stopping episode, not a tutorial slide deck, and it sells the pack on the product page and on Reels/Shorts/TikTok.

## Customizations

- Original characters: Juno (low-poly maker, mint hoodie) and Pixel (a coral block creature with four stubby legs, nub arms and black rectangle eyes that change shape to show emotion).
