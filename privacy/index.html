1. Introduction

PromptPack (“we”, “our”, “us”) is operated by Ornalens LLP, India. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it when you visit promptpack.tech or buy the AI Money Audit Prompt Pack. By using the site you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Purchase details: your name, email address and phone number as entered at checkout, used to process your order and deliver your download.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. We receive only confirmation of payment (order ID, payment ID, amount and status).
  • Usage and device data: pages visited, clicks, approximate location, browser and device type, and the campaign or link that brought you here (for example UTM parameters), collected through cookies and similar technologies.

3. What we do not collect

The prompts run in the AI tool you choose. We never receive the financial information (such as transactions, balances, statements or subscriptions) that you paste into your own AI tool. We do not ask for your bank login and do not connect to your accounts. Please read your AI provider’s privacy terms before sharing sensitive information with it.

4. How we use information

  • To process your payment, deliver your download and provide support.
  • To send service messages about your order.
  • To understand how the site is used and to measure and improve our advertising.
  • To prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.

5. Analytics and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity. The Meta Pixel lets us measure ads on Facebook and Instagram and see generic events such as page views and checkout clicks. Microsoft Clarity records aggregated interaction data such as clicks and scrolling to help us improve the page. We do not send your financial information to Meta or Microsoft. You can limit tracking through your browser settings, ad preferences on Meta, or a content blocker.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run the store:

  • Razorpay — payment processing.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Meta Platforms and Microsoft — advertising measurement and site analytics, as described above.
  • An email or file-delivery provider, if we use one to send your download link.

We may also disclose information if required by law.
