Carousel Design vs Publishing Workflow: Where to Start

By PromptPack · Product facts reviewed 28 October 2026. Prices and inclusions reflect the current catalog.

Most carousel problems are one of two kinds: the slides do not look like one connected piece, or finished slides get stuck between draft, approval and posting. Carousel Studio covers both, as two routes in one download. Start with the route that matches your problem.

What is the difference between the two routes?

Both routes come in one download: a 29-page guide and 28 editable prompts in total, for US$15 / ₹899 once.

What does the download include?

  • 13-page design guide and 16-page workflow guide (illustrated PDFs)
  • 28 prompts: 12 for design, 16 for planning and approval
  • Master brief, reference, slide, approval and review worksheets
  • Six-slide editorial carousel example with caption, alt text and sources
  • Daily folder planner, result ranking and an official Instagram publishing starter

Which route should you start with?

Start with design if your slides do not look connected. Define one audience, one message and shared typography and image direction. Finish a coherent carousel before adding scheduling.

Start with the workflow if content is ready but approvals are disorganized. Give each post an owner, revision and daily folder. Approve the exact files before connecting a publishing route. The workflow does not create your illustrations.

How do the two routes work together?

For an illustrative desk-lamp checklist, use the design route to assign one useful job to each of six slides and keep the lighting, palette and headline hierarchy consistent. Then, in the workflow route, record the revision, assemble final images and caption, obtain approval and prepare the publishing package. This is a suggested exercise, not a completed customer campaign.

Does the kit publish automatically to every platform?

Image generation needs your own tool, account and credits. The optional starter renderer only checks text fit; it does not generate illustrations. The workflow tools run locally: the Instagram publishing starter is mock-tested and needs your own Meta app, review and credentials. No hosted scheduler or automatic publishing is included, and the examples show an editorial process, not guaranteed reach or engagement.

What should you inspect before buying?

Try the free sample and inspect the worked-example slides on the Carousel Studio page. Tool accounts, image credits and any hosting costs are separate.

Want to try the method before buying anything? The free six-slide carousel template shows a complete worked example: the filled prompt, the plan it produced, the slides and the revisions.
