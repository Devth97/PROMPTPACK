Digital products and small-business offers

Explain fixed USD and INR prices consistently

The display order can change while the actual regional offers remain fixed.

Written by PromptPack · Guide 95 of 100 · Fictional example

The methodWorked exampleReview checklistOptional AI prompt

Start with the task and the source

Clarify what you sell, show the actual files and test the buying experience. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. The worked example below shows one way to organise the task; replace its assumptions only with information you have checked.

  1. Write down the input and the question you want to resolve.
  2. Separate confirmed facts from missing or ambiguous information.
  3. Follow the example with your own verified inputs, preserving units, scope and source references.
  4. Complete the review check before sending, publishing or acting on the result.
Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

A fictional product has fixed regional offers of US$19 and INR 999. A draft changes the rupee figure using a live exchange rate, causing a mismatch with checkout.

What to do

Keep the supplied values unchanged. Present US$19 as the large primary price, followed by or INR 999 in smaller text. Explain one-time payment only if that is the actual arrangement. Do not imply the two fixed offers are a currency conversion.

What the result means

Compare cards, product details and checkout summaries with the server-owned amounts. Check both currency selections in the real flow before release. Display consistency is a presentation change, not authority to alter the charge.

Check before you use it

Compare both labels with the server-owned checkout amounts.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

Using these exact fixed prices [USD amount] and [INR amount], draft a product-card price label with USD first and a smaller or INR alternative. State one-time payment only if confirmed. Do not calculate exchange rates, invent discounts or change the amounts to make them appear equivalent.
