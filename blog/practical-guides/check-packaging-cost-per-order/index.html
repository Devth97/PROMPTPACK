Bakery costing and customer quotes

Check packaging cost per order

Boxes and boards can be counted differently from individual baked items.

Written by PromptPack · Guide 4 of 100 · Fictional example

The methodWorked exampleReview checklistOptional AI prompt

Start with the task and the source

Turn recipe notes into a cost picture you can review before sending a quote. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. The worked example below shows one way to organise the task; replace its assumptions only with information you have checked.

  1. Write down the input and the question you want to resolve.
  2. Separate confirmed facts from missing or ambiguous information.
  3. Follow the example with your own verified inputs, preserving units, scope and source references.
  4. Complete the review check before sending, publishing or acting on the result.
Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

A fictional order contains 24 cookies, packed six to a box. Each box costs 20, each cookie sleeve costs 2 and one outer carrier costs 10.

What to do

Four boxes are needed: 24 ÷ 6 = 4. Boxes cost 80, sleeves cost 48 and the order-level carrier costs 10. Distinguish the quantities instead of applying every packaging cost to each cookie.

What the result means

The packaging total is 138. Check whether labels, inserts or protective materials also belong in this order. A packaging unit described as one pack must be converted to the actual number of usable pieces before costing.

Check before you use it

Reconcile the number of boxes with their capacity and the order quantity.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

Create a packaging checklist for [order description]. Use only these quantities and costs: [records]. Distinguish per-item wrapping, per-box packaging and one-off boards. Calculate totals and mark missing items as unknown rather than free.
