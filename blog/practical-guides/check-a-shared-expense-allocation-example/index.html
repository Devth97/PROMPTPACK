Check a shared-expense allocation example

Arithmetic can be checked independently of whether an allocation was agreed.

Start with the task and the source

Use fictional records to design neutral worksheets; keep personal and family information private. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. The worked example below shows one way to organise the task; replace its assumptions only with information you have checked.

  1. Write down the input and the question you want to resolve.
  2. Separate confirmed facts from missing or ambiguous information.
  3. Follow the example with your own verified inputs, preserving units, scope and source references.
  4. Complete the review check before sending, publishing or acting on the result.
Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

A fictional expense totals 120 in one currency. The supplied example rule allocates 60% to role A and 40% to role B. It is explicitly a calculation exercise.

What to do

Multiply 120 by 0.60 to get 72 and by 0.40 to get 48. Check that the percentages total 100% and that 72 + 48 returns 120. Keep the stated rule attached to the calculation.

What the result means

The arithmetic is correct for the supplied example. Verify the actual agreed allocation separately before applying it to real records. Do not treat a worksheet result as establishing legal responsibility, consent or a payment already made.

Check before you use it

The example shares are 72 and 48; verify the rule separately.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

For a fictional expense of [120] with a supplied agreed split of [60/40], show each share and reconciliation to the total. Keep currencies separate. Explain that the calculation applies only to the entered rule and does not establish who is legally responsible for payment.
