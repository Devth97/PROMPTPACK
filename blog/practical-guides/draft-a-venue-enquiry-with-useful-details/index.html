Wedding planning and vendor comparisons

Draft a venue enquiry with useful details

A specific enquiry helps a venue explain its actual offer.

Written by PromptPack · Guide 72 of 100 · Fictional example

The methodWorked exampleReview checklistOptional AI prompt

Start with the task and the source

Compare supplied facts and organise decisions while checking actual vendor terms yourself. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. The worked example below shows one way to organise the task; replace its assumptions only with information you have checked.

  1. Write down the input and the question you want to resolve.
  2. Separate confirmed facts from missing or ambiguous information.
  3. Follow the example with your own verified inputs, preserving units, scope and source references.
  4. Complete the review check before sending, publishing or acting on the result.
Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

A fictional couple is considering an evening event for about eighty guests. They have a proposed date but no confirmed arrival time, food plan or accessibility information.

What to do

Send a focused enquiry asking for availability, usable spaces, access hours, the guest basis for the quote and the venue's actual accessibility information. Keep personal guest records out of the initial message and request written inclusions and conditions.

What the result means

The response can be compared with another venue using the same questions. Verify the real event details before requesting a binding quote. An enquiry should not be presented as a reservation or a confirmed commitment.

Check before you use it

Replace example dates and confirm what the written quote actually covers.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

Draft a venue enquiry for [fictional date, approximate guest count and event format]. Ask about availability, included spaces, access times, food arrangements, accessibility information and written quote terms. Do not share guest identities or assume a listed package includes every required service.
