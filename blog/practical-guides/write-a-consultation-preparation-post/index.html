Salon content and booking conversations

Write a consultation preparation post

Help customers arrive with useful questions rather than a promised result.

Written by PromptPack · Guide 16 of 100 · Fictional example

The methodWorked exampleReview checklistOptional AI prompt

Start with the task and the source

Create practical content from your real services, hours and filming capacity. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. The worked example below shows one way to organise the task; replace its assumptions only with information you have checked.

  1. Write down the input and the question you want to resolve.
  2. Separate confirmed facts from missing or ambiguous information.
  3. Follow the example with your own verified inputs, preserving units, scope and source references.
  4. Complete the review check before sending, publishing or acting on the result.
Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

A fictional salon wants customers to bring reference ideas to a colour consultation. The provider says maintenance expectations and time available are useful discussion points.

What to do

Organise the post into goals, references, upkeep questions and appointment planning. Ask clients to discuss what they want rather than promising the exact result in a photograph. Keep any suitability or condition-specific advice with the qualified provider.

What the result means

The finished post helps a customer prepare a conversation. It does not diagnose hair condition or say a service will suit everyone. Ask the provider to approve the wording before using it as a public service explainer.

Check before you use it

Remove any recommendation the qualified service provider has not approved.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

Draft a carousel outline explaining how to prepare for a [service] consultation. Use these salon-approved notes [notes]. Suggest questions about goals, upkeep, time and costs. Do not assess a person's condition, prescribe treatment or guarantee the result from a reference photo.
