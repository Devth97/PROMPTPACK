Write voice direction without imitation

Describe delivery in ordinary terms that a performer can use.

Written by PromptPack · Guide 47 of 100 · Fictional example

The methodWorked exampleReview checklistOptional AI prompt

Start with the task and the source

Prepare a story, shots and review notes before spending time on production. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. The worked example below shows one way to organise the task; replace its assumptions only with information you have checked.

  1. Write down the input and the question you want to resolve.
  2. Separate confirmed facts from missing or ambiguous information.
  3. Follow the example with your own verified inputs, preserving units, scope and source references.
  4. Complete the review check before sending, publishing or acting on the result.
Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

Two original characters have similar dialogue lengths but different intentions: one is excited to demonstrate a tool; the other wants to check the instructions before trying it.

What to do

Describe the first delivery as brisk and curious, the second as measured with a short pause before the question. Add pronunciation guidance for unusual terms. Avoid asking the performer or synthesis tool to copy a named actor's voice.

What the result means

Listen to the whole conversation for intelligibility, pacing and relative loudness. Clear character distinction comes from consistent direction and writing, not only a pitch change. Revise the text if the delivery needs to rush to fit the scene.

Check before you use it

Listen to the complete scene for clarity, pronunciation and balanced levels.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

Write voice direction for this original dialogue [dialogue]. Specify pace, emotional intention, pauses and pronunciation notes. Give character distinctions through delivery rather than copying a named actor or real person's voice. Keep the instructions consistent with the meaning of each line.
