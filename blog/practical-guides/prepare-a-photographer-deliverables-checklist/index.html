Compare supplied facts and organise decisions while checking actual vendor terms yourself. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

A fictional photography package names two photographers and eight hours but leaves delivery format and image-use permissions unclear. The couple assumes every raw file is included.

What to do

Separate attendance, editing, final file format, delivery timing and permissions. Ask whether raw files are included rather than adding them to the expected deliverables. Keep ownership and publication questions tied to the actual agreement.

What the result means

The clarification checklist helps align expectations with the supplied package. Request written answers before finalising the record. Do not assume payment grants unlimited use, or that a supplier may publish all images without checking the relevant arrangements.

Check before you use it

Confirm the final deliverables and image-use permissions in writing.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

Create a clarification checklist from this fictional photography proposal [proposal]. Cover attendance hours, photographer count, editing scope, delivery format, delivery timing, usage terms and stated extra charges. Preserve unknowns.
