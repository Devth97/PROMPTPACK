Plan a product unboxing shot sequence

A clear sequence shows what is actually included.

Written by PromptPack · Guide 54 of 100 · Fictional example

The methodWorked exampleReview checklistOptional AI prompt

Start with the task and the source

Build a script around demonstrable product facts and footage you can actually produce. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. The worked example below shows one way to organise the task; replace its assumptions only with information you have checked.

  1. Write down the input and the question you want to resolve.
  2. Separate confirmed facts from missing or ambiguous information.
  3. Follow the example with your own verified inputs, preserving units, scope and source references.
  4. Complete the review check before sending, publishing or acting on the result.
Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

A fictional package contains a guide, two printed cards and one holder. A decorative pen appears in the proposed unboxing scene but is sold separately.

What to do

Show the actual inventory in order and label optional props clearly. If a prop could be mistaken for an inclusion, remove it or explain the distinction in the shot. Use the delivered packaging rather than an invented premium box.

What the result means

The unboxing accurately represents what a buyer receives. Compare the final footage with a real customer order and inspect quick cuts, captions and thumbnails: misleading inclusion cues can remain even when the spoken script is correct.

Check before you use it

Compare the shot plan with a real order before filming.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

Create an unboxing shot list from this verified package inventory [list]. Show outer packaging, each included item and relevant setup steps. Distinguish optional accessories from included items. Suggest clean framing and readable labels without adding branded elements or packaging that the delivered product lacks.
