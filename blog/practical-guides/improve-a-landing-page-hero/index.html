Improve a landing-page hero

Help a visitor understand the offer and the next action quickly.

Start with the task and the source

Give an AI coding tool specific requirements and review the result one section at a time. Use the original record, approved brief or supplied specification as your starting point. The worked example below shows one way to organise the task; replace its assumptions only with information you have checked.

  1. Write down the input and the question you want to resolve.
  2. Separate confirmed facts from missing or ambiguous information.
  3. Follow the example with your own verified inputs, preserving units, scope and source references.
  4. Complete the review check before sending, publishing or acting on the result.
Fictional worked example

Work through the example

The starting point

A fictional product page opens with Unlock Your Potential. The actual offer is an editable checklist and example workbook for comparing wedding vendor quotes.

What to do

Replace the vague promise with a concrete task: Compare wedding quotes before you choose. Add a sentence about the actual workbook, example and required desktop software. Use a primary action that opens the real product details or purchase route.

What the result means

The revised opening explains the offer without a savings guarantee. Review the heading beside the cover image: a buyer should understand the digital format, the task and the next step without inferring a physical planner will arrive.

Check before you use it

Check that the headline describes something the actual product delivers.

Keep the original source available so you can compare it with the draft. Leave unresolved questions visible rather than filling them with guessed facts. An AI-generated draft is an aid to review; it does not perform a payment, send a message, publish content or establish a professional conclusion.

Optional prompt to organise your draft

Replace the bracketed placeholders with fictional or anonymised information. Do not paste credentials, payment details, client records or confidential family documents. Use the prompt to prepare a draft, then check it against the method above.

Review this hero copy [copy] for [audience]. Rewrite it with a concrete headline, one explanatory sentence and a clear primary action. Use only the supplied product facts. Offer three directions with different emphasis, and explain what each one helps the visitor understand.
