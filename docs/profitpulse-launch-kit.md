## A demonstration to record

Use the free calculator for the first public demonstration. Its behavior can be checked independently; the marketing illustrations are not screenshots of actual customer results. These are demonstration instructions, not a claim that a recording already exists.

### A simple, reproducible example

Enter these fictional inputs:

| Input | Value |
|---|---:|
| Selling price | INR 1,000 |
| Landed product cost | INR 400 |
| Forward shipping | INR 50 |
| Packaging | INR 10 |
| Estimated acquisition cost | INR 100 |
| Reverse shipping and COD fee | 0 |
| Every percentage field, including tax and fees | 0 |

Expected contribution before ads: `1000 - 400 - 50 - 10 = INR 540`. After the entered acquisition cost: **INR 440**. Modelled break-even ROAS: `1000 / 540`, or **1.85x**. This deliberately simplified example excludes fixed costs, tax, fees and failed deliveries; zero is an example input, not a recommendation to ignore those costs.

Change forward shipping from 50 to 100. Contribution before ads becomes **INR 490**, after entered CAC **INR 390**, and modelled break-even ROAS **2.04x**. State that the ROAS measure uses nominal selling price per placed order. Do not compare it directly with a platform's reported ROAS without reconciling definitions.

### Record these fields for each experiment

Experiment ID; start/end dates in Asia/Calcutta; hypothesis; creative and destination URL; price; audience definition; total spend cap; actual spend; visits; checkout starts where measured; captured paid orders; refunds; net receipts; variable fulfillment/support costs; unresolved issues; next decision.

An unmeasured count stays **unknown**, not zero. Use the same attribution window when comparing results. A visit to checkout is not a captured payment.

### Paid-test decision arithmetic

Use actual net receipts after applicable taxes, payment charges and refunds. Subtract variable fulfillment/support costs and the contribution you want to retain. The remainder is a provisional acquisition-cost allowance, not a recommended bid. A price of INR 999 does not mean there are INR 999 available for ads. Tax and fee treatment must come from your actual setup and adviser.

Do not buy traffic while successful delivery is unproven, unresolved errors prevent first use, or the spending cap is undefined.
